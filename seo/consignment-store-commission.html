Consignment Store Commission Explained

Store commission is the store's percentage; the consignor share is the remainder.

Keep the labels consistent

With a 40% store commission, the consignor split is 60% of the net sale. On a $95 net sale, the store earns $38 and the consignor earns $57. Use the free calculator to test other rates and fees.
