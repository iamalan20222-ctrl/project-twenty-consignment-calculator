How the math works

Net sale equals sale price minus eligible fees. Store earnings equal net sale multiplied by the store commission percentage. Consignor earnings are the remaining net sale. For example, a $100 sale, 40% store commission, and $5 fee produces $38 for the store and $57 for the consignor.
