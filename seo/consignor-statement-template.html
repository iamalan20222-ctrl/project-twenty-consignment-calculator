Consignor Statement Template

A useful statement lets a consignor see which items sold and how the payout was calculated.

Statement checklist

Include the consignor name or ID, statement period, item ID, item description, sale date, sale price, eligible fee, store commission, consignor payout, and whether payment is pending or paid. Keep the statement tied to the same item IDs used in inventory and sales records.
