PAYOUT RECONCILIATION

Consignor Payment Tracking Checklist

A payout record should answer what was owed, what was paid, and which sold items support it.

Use four fields

Record payout amount, payment status, payment date, and statement period alongside the item or consignor ID. Review sold-but-unpaid rows before each closeout. For the arithmetic, open the free payout calculator; for a linked inventory-to-statement workflow, see the tracker workbook.
