FEE-AWARE PAYOUTS

Consignment Payout After Fees

When an agreement deducts fees before the split, show the fee and the net sale separately.

Worked example

A $100 sale minus a $5 fee gives a $95 net sale. With a 40% store commission, store earnings are $38 and consignor earnings are $57. The percentages apply to the net sale, not the original sticker price.
