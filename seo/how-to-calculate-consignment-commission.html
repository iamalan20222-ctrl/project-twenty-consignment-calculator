How to Calculate Consignment Commission

Use a repeatable sequence: define the fee base, calculate net sale, then split the remainder.

Three steps

  1. Subtract eligible fees from the sale price.
  2. Multiply the net sale by the store commission percentage.
  3. Subtract store earnings from net sale to get the consignor payout.

With a $100 sale, $5 fee, and 40% store commission: net sale is $95, store earnings are $38, and consignor payout is $57.

Keep the record explainable
