How to Calculate Consignor Payouts

If fees are deducted before the split, calculate net sale as sale price minus eligible fees. Consignor payout is net sale multiplied by the consignor percentage. A $100 sale with $5 in fees and a 40% share produces a $95 net sale, a $38 consignor payout, and $57 for the store.
