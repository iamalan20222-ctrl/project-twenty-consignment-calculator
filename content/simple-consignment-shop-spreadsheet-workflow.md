# A Simple Consignment Shop Spreadsheet Workflow

Use four linked sheets: Inventory, Sales, Consignors, and Payouts. At intake, assign an item ID and capture location. At sale, enter the item ID, price, date, and fee. At closeout, filter sold items by consignor, calculate each payout, and mark payment status.

Use the [free payout calculator](https://iamalan20222-ctrl.github.io/project-twenty-consignment-calculator/) for a quick check, or get the [complete tracker workbook](https://payhip.com/b/QaUOC?utm_source=content_workflow&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=traffic001).
