# How to Track Consignment Inventory Without Expensive Software

Start with one row per item. Give each item a stable ID, then record the consignor, date received, asking price, location, and status: Active, Sold, Returned, or Expired.

When an item sells, update the sale date and price immediately. The important control is the link between item ID and consignor. Without that link, a sales total can look correct while the payout is wrong.
